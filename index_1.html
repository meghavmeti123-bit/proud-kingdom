📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Drive along the highway and reach your target distance before time expires. Avoid traffic collisions, overtake safely, and collect coins on the asphalt!

⌨️ DESKTOP / LAPTOP CONTROLS

  • A / Left Arrow : Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow : Steer Right
  • W / Up Arrow : Accelerate (Speed Up)
  • S / Down Arrow / Space : Brake (Slow Down)
  • P / ESC : Pause / Resume Game
  • R : Quick Restart after Game Over

📱 MOBILE TOUCH CONTROLS

  • LEFT / RIGHT : Hold to steer smoothly between lanes
  • ACCELERATE : Hold to boost car speed
  • BRAKE : Hold to brake and avoid collisions

❤️ HEALTH & TRAFFIC RULES

You have 3 Health points. Hitting traffic slows you down and deals damage. When health reaches 0 or time runs out, the race ends!
